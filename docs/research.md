# Market review and writing guidance

A review of popular resume builders and recruiter guidance, made to decide what NeatCV should offer. Reviewed in October 2026.

## What the leading builders do well

| Builder | Strength worth learning from | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Rezi | Keyword targeting against a job description | PDF downloads limited on the free plan |
| Teal | Matching mode that lists the posting’s keywords; job tracker | Full keyword list is paid |
| Jobscan | Match rate by hard skills, title, education and soft skills | Five scans a month for free |
| Enhancv | Real-time content feedback, flexible section control | Seven-day free access |
| Kickresume | Separate design and content grades, many templates, TXT export | Limited free templates |
| Zety, Resume Genius, MyPerfectResume | Pre-written phrase libraries and step-by-step guidance | PDF download is paid |
| Resume.io | Clean templates, match score with a gap list | Free plan downloads TXT only |
| Novorésumé | Content optimizer that flags phrasing and completeness | Free plan: one page, fixed layouts |
| FlowCV | Free unlimited PDFs, section ordering, colors and spacing | Fonts and themes partly paid |
| Reactive Resume | Open source; drag-and-drop section order, custom sections | Self-hosting or account needed |

What NeatCV adopted, all free and without an account:

- a final review with concrete, fixable checks (Enhancv, Novorésumé, Kickresume)
- vacancy matching with covered and missing skills (Teal, Jobscan, Rezi) — computed in the browser
- writing guidance, examples, and an action-verb library (Zety, Resume Genius)
- section ordering and hiding (Reactive Resume, FlowCV)
- more templates and colors, an optional photo for European applications (Kickresume, Novorésumé)
- plain-text export for application forms (Resume.io, Zety)

What it deliberately does not do: generate achievements, add hidden keywords, or show a single “ATS score”. Screening systems differ, and an invented number would mislead.

## Writing guidance used in the checks

- **Achievements over duties.** Use Laszlo Bock’s formula: “Accomplished X as measured by Y by doing Z.” Replace openers like “responsible for” or “helped”.
- **Numbers.** Aim for a measurable result in at least half of the experience points: percentages, money, time, users, team size.
- **Length.** Three to six points for a recent role, two or three for older ones; one or two lines per point. One to two pages overall.
- **Profile.** Two to four sentences: who you are, your strongest proof with a number, what you are looking for.
- **Skills.** Concrete tools and methods, written as the vacancy writes them, and only the ones you have. Prove the key ones in your experience.
- **Format.** Single column, standard section names, reverse chronological order, real text rather than images.
- **Photo.** Expected by most recruiters in Germany, Austria and Switzerland; usually omitted in the US, UK and Canada.
